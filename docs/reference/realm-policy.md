# Historical realm policy

**Diataxis category:** historical reference.

Realm and Gateway policy documents describe the retired hierarchy. Current
authorization is Zone-scoped and binds the authenticated peer to User,
RoleBinding, Resource, Provider, generation, and revision state.

Use the current [Zone CLI contract](./zone-cli-contract.md), [Zone Nix
authoring](./zone-control-nix.md), and [privileges reference](./privileges.md)
for new integrations. Historical policy text cannot authorize a current
operation or justify a fallback.
